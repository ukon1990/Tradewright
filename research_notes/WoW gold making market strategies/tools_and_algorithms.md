# Existing WoW gold-making tools (addons and websites): features and algorithms, as of 2026-11-20

Source tiers used below: [peer-reviewed], [working paper], [official], [primary data], [source code], [tool docs], [reference wiki], [community].
Every link below was opened during this research (2026-11-19/20) unless it sits in a Gaps section. Where a description of an algorithm comes from a third-party fork or reimplementation instead of the tool's own code or docs, that is stated on the line.

## 1. TradeSkillMaster (TSM): market value, price sources, operations, groups, Ledger

### Takeaway
TSM's documented "market value" is a per-scan trimmed mean (lowest 15-30% of listings, cut at a 20% price jump, then drop values beyond 1.5 standard deviations) blended into a recency-weighted 14-day average; everything else (historical, region, sale rate, sold per day) is derived from that or from pooled Accounting data that a single-client addon cannot reproduce. Its workflow model is "groups of items + operations attached to groups", with a mail-driven Ledger for realized buys and sells.

### Cited Findings

**Market value (dbmarket) algorithm**
- Step 1: only the lowest 30% of auctions are considered at most; after the first 15% of auctions, a price increase of 20% or more between consecutive auctions ends the data set (that auction and everything above it are discarded); jumps before the 15% mark do not trigger the cutoff. [tool docs] — [TSM support: How is AuctionDB Market Value calculated](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- Step 2: compute mean and standard deviation of the survivors and discard any price more than 1.5 standard deviations from the mean. Step 3: the average of what remains is the market value for that scan. [tool docs] — [same article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- Step 4: scans from the same day are averaged into one daily value; the final market value is a weighted average of the previous 14 days plus today, weighted toward recent days, with a stated "half life" of a little over 2 days. The exact daily weights appear only in an image in the article. [tool docs] — [same article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- Worked example in the article: {5, 13, 13, 15, 15, 15, 16, 17, 17, 19, ...} keeps {5, 13, 13, 15, 15, 15, 16} after step 1, drops 5 in step 2, and gives a scan value of 14.5. [tool docs] — [same article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- A third-party WotLK 3.3.5a backport of TSM v4.14.66 implements the same steps in Lua with constants `MIN_PERCENTILE = 0.15`, `MAX_PERCENTILE = 0.30`, `MAX_JUMP = 1.2`, a population standard deviation and a `mean ± 1.5 * stdDev` keep-window; it protects at least the first 2 prices from the jump rule and skips the deviation filter when fewer than 3 prices survive. This is the backporter's reimplementation, not TSM's own published code. [source code, third-party reimplementation] — [Keoo88 backport: AuctionScan/Classes/Util.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Keoo88/TradeSkillMaster-3.3.5-backport/HEAD/TradeSkillMaster/LibTSMService/Source/AuctionScan/Classes/Util.lua)
- The same backport stores per-item daily snapshot averages for 15 days and daily market values for 60 days, and weights days 0..14 with `{132, 125, 100, 75, 45, 34, 33, 38, 28, 21, 15, 10, 7, 5, 4}`, which its code comment calls the "Original TSM DBMarket weights". The official article does not print these numbers, so treat them as an unverified third-party claim. [source code, third-party reimplementation] — [Keoo88 backport: TradeSkillMaster_AuctionDB/Core.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Keoo88/TradeSkillMaster-3.3.5-backport/HEAD/TradeSkillMaster_AuctionDB/Core.lua)

**Price sources (gold values)**
- DBMarket: "a weighted 14-day average of the auctions listed for a particular item on your realms Auction House", favoring the most recent 3 days; it reflects listings, not sales. [tool docs] — [TSM support: Which Price Sources can I use](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- DBMinBuyout: the lowest priced auction on the realm as of the last pricing update; it is not live and may be invalid when no auctions exist. DBRecent: like market value but from the latest snapshot only. DBHistorical: a 60-day average of DBMarket. [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- DBRegionMarketAvg: the average of DBMarket across all realms in the region. DBRegionHistorical: a 60-day average of DBRegionMarketAvg. DBRegionSaleAvg: the average sale and purchase price in the region, collected from TSM Accounting data via the TSM Desktop Application. [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Crafting: the sum of the matPrice of each material in the recipe (requires that you have the recipe). MatPrice: the cost of a material; only items used by a known recipe have one. [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Destroy: the average value of what you receive by destroying the item (disenchant/mill/prospect), by default valued at DBMarket. VendorBuy: populated only after visiting a vendor that sells the item. VendorSell: vendor sale value. [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Accounting sources: AvgBuy / AvgSell (average over all your purchases / sales of the item), MinBuy / MaxBuy / MinSell / MaxSell, and SmartAvgBuy (average purchase price over your last X purchases, where X is the quantity you currently hold; invalid if you hold none). [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Operation sources: AuctioningOpMin / Max / Normal, ShoppingOpMax, SniperOpMax expose the prices from the item's group operations. DBRegionMinBuyoutAvg has been retired. [tool docs] — [same page](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)

**Value sources (numbers, not gold)**
- DBRegionSaleRate: a decimal sale rate averaged across all auction houses in the region; 0.4 means the item sells about 40% of the times it is posted. DBRegionSoldPerDay: the volume sold on average per auction house per day in the region. [tool docs] — [TSM support: Which Value Sources can I use](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/custom-strings/which-value-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Personal equivalents: SaleRate (your own sale rate for the item), NumExpires (times the item expired or was cancelled since it last sold), NumInventory (quantity you own), plus ItemLevel, ItemQuality, RequiredLevel. [tool docs] — [same page](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/custom-strings/which-value-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)

**Groups and operations**
- Groups are "containers for a collection of items you wish to handle in specific ways"; groups can hold sub-groups; the Base Group contains every item not added to another group and cannot be removed; importing always creates a new group named "Imported Group"; export can include sub-groups and optionally operations. [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Groups](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-groups)
- Operations are "instructions applied to a Group that determine how TSM handles items within those groups". Types: Auctioning (posting), Crafting (queue building), Mailing (send to characters), Shopping (surface items to buy), Sniper (surface items as they are posted), Vendoring (sell to vendor), Warehousing (move between bags and bank / guild bank). [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Managing Operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-manging-operations)
- Auctioning settings: duration 12/24/48 h, post cap, amount kept in bags, "don't post after this many expires", bid percent, undercut amount (default 0c on Retail, 1c on Classic), minimum / maximum / normal price (normal is the fallback when there is no competition), configurable actions when competition is below minimum or above maximum (don't post, post at min/max/normal, ignore auctions below min), ignore low-duration auctions (Short under 30 min, Medium 30 min-2 h, Long 2-12 h), blacklist, cancel undercut auctions (default yes; on Retail also when someone posts the same price after you), cancel to repost higher with a repost threshold. [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Auctioning Operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/tsm-addon-documentation/1072146-tsm-addon-auctioning-operations)
- Crafting settings: min restock quantity (minimum number that must be queued before anything is added), max restock quantity (target stock), minimum profit (enabled by default at 100g), optional craft value override; restock values must resolve between 0 and 2000. "Crafted Item Value minus Crafting Cost is the profit", with crafting cost the sum of material prices. [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Crafting Operations](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/1236068-tsm-addon-crafting-operations)
- Shopping settings: maximum auction price (custom string), show auctions above max price (on by default), maximum restock quantity, inventory sources counted for restock. [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Shopping Operations](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-shopping-operations)
- Shipped defaults: Shopping max price `dbmarket` with no restock limit; Crafting min restock 10 / max restock 20 / min profit 100g; Vendoring requires 20 expires and excludes soulbound items; Warehousing default does nothing; Auctioning and Sniper defaults are listed as "coming soon". [tool docs] — [How do the #Default Operations work?](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/addon/how-do-the-default-operations-work)
- Sniper: on Retail it behaves like a Shopping operation whose search is repeated until stopped, with "a 30 second delay between each search"; on Classic it repeatedly searches the last page of the auction house (the newest postings). The main setting is a maximum price custom string. It does not run on the Base Group in Retail but does in Classic. [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Sniper Operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-sniper-operations)

**Ledger / Accounting**
- The Ledger is built from mail (sales, purchases, cancelled and expired auctions). Sections: Inventory (quantities per character bags/bank/mail, alts, guild banks, active auctions, with a total value from a configurable price source), Revenue (Sales by Auction / COD / Trade / Vendor; Other income such as mission rewards and transfers; Resale), Expenses (Purchases; Other such as postage and repairs), Failed Auctions (Expired, Cancelled). [tool docs] — [TSM Addon: Ledger](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-ledger)
- Resale shows, per item with both buys and sells: quantity bought and average buy price, quantity sold and average sale price, average profit per item and profit %, and optional total profit. Item drill-down shows quantity, average and total price for 7 days / 30 days / all time and the most frequent counterparties. [tool docs] — [same page](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/en_US/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-ledger)

**Client support and maintenance**
- CurseForge lists TSM for Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Classic and Classic TBC; latest file v4.15.8 on 2026-11-18; about 64.6M downloads. Listed features: groups/operations/import-export and Ledger, Crafting window (profit, queues, gathering), task list, Destroying window, Mailing, Vendoring, bank UI. [tool docs] — [CurseForge: TradeSkillMaster](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tradeskill-master)
- Other addons read TSM through `TSM_API.ToItemString(link)` and `TSM_API.GetCustomPriceValue(customString, itemString)`; CraftSim reads sale rate with the string `dbregionsalerate*1000` because fractional results round down to nothing. [source code] — [CraftSim DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/main/DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua)

### Inferences
- For an addon limited to its own scans, only the per-scan trimmed mean, the daily average, the 14-day weighted blend and a 60-day "historical" are reproducible. Region averages, sale rate and sold-per-day depend on TSM's pooled data and must be replaced by local estimates (see section 5).
- The price-jump rule is what protects the estimate from a wall of overpriced listings; the 1.5 standard deviation step mainly removes very low "bait" listings (as in the worked example where 5 is dropped).
- The Auctioning min / normal / max triplet with explicit "below min / above max" actions is the core of TSM's posting safety and is cheap to copy as a design.

### Gaps
- TSM's own addon source is not in a public first-party repository that I could find; the Lua shown above is from a third-party backport.
- How TSM computes DBRegionSaleRate and DBRegionSoldPerDay (inputs, time window) is not stated on the pages opened; only the definitions are.
- The official per-day weights for the 14-day average are only in an image; the numbers above come from third-party code.
- Mailing, Vendoring and Warehousing operation setting pages were not opened; only their one-line descriptions are sourced.
- How operations are inherited or overridden by sub-groups, and the per-group operation limit, are not covered by the pages opened.

## 2. Auctionator: storage, shopping lists, crafting info, undercut logic, public price API

### Takeaway
Auctionator keeps a very small price model: per item key it stores the last seen minimum price plus, per day, the lowest and highest "minimum price" and the highest quantity seen, and its public API simply returns that last seen minimum. Its value to players is speed and simplicity of buying, posting and cancelling, not statistics.

### Cited Findings
- CurseForge lists Auctionator for Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Titan Reforged Classic and Classic; last update 2026-11-18; about 215.4M downloads. Described features: auction prices in tooltips (updated by a full scan), shopping searches with filters, history and lists, undercut scan with one-click cancelling, crafting info showing reagent cost and profit with a button to search reagents, protection against posting too low, autoscan on Retail when the AH opens. [tool docs] — [CurseForge: Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator)
- The upstream GitHub repository URL `github.com/Auctionator/Auctionator` returned HTTP 404 on 2026-11-19, and its wiki pages also returned 404; the organisation still hosts side projects. [primary data] — [GitHub API: Auctionator org repositories](https://api.github.com/users/Auctionator/repos?per_page=50)
- The code facts below come from a public third-party fork of Auctionator 9.1.3 (TOC `Interface: 30300`, a private-server port), not from the current upstream. [source code, third-party fork] — [sirus-addons/Auctionator Auctionator.toc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Auctionator.toc)
- Price database record per key: `l` = lowest low price on a given day, `h` = highest low price on a given day, `a` = highest quantity seen on a given day, `m` = last seen minimum price. `ProcessScan` takes the minimum price over all listings of the key and sums the available quantity; `l` is only stored when it differs from `h` to save memory. [source code, third-party fork] — [Source/Database/Mixin.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Source/Database/Mixin.lua)
- History is keyed by day number since a fixed epoch, pruned after a configurable number of days (`PRICE_HISTORY_DAYS`), and `GetPriceHistory` returns per day: date, minSeen, maxSeen, available. [source code, third-party fork] — [same file](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Source/Database/Mixin.lua)
- Saved variables in that version: `AUCTIONATOR_PRICE_DATABASE`, `AUCTIONATOR_POSTING_HISTORY`, `AUCTIONATOR_SHOPPING_LISTS`, `AUCTIONATOR_VENDOR_PRICE_CACHE`, plus config. [source code, third-party fork] — [Auctionator.toc](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Auctionator.toc)
- Public API (documented as stable): `Auctionator.API.v1.GetAuctionPriceByItemID(callerID, itemID)` and `Auctionator.API.v1.GetAuctionPriceByItemLink(callerID, itemLink)` return "the last scanned price" in copper or nil; `Auctionator.API.v1.MultiSearch(callerID, terms)` runs a list of searches and requires the AH to be open. Calling anything else is unsupported. [source code, third-party fork] — [Source/API/v1/README.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Source/API/v1/README.md)
- Implementation: the ItemID variant returns `Database:GetPrice(tostring(itemID))` (the `m` field); the ItemLink variant resolves a list of database keys from the link (item-level aware) and returns the first key that has a price, falling back to a basic key. `callerID` must be a string and is validated. [source code, third-party fork] — [Source/API/v1/GetAuctionPrice.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Source/API/v1/GetAuctionPrice.lua)
- Current consumers also call `Auctionator.API.v1.GetVendorPriceByItemID(callerID, itemID)` and `GetVendorPriceByItemLink(callerID, itemLink)` and prefer the vendor price when one exists, which shows these functions exist in the current API. [source code] — [CraftSim DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/main/DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua)
- Undercut logic when selling: the suggested price is either `basePrice * (100 - undercutPercent) / 100` or `basePrice - staticAmount`, chosen by a setting, never below 100 copper (1 silver). There are two independent setting sets, one for "LIFO" items and one for "not LIFO" items. [source code, third-party fork] — [Source/Selling/LIFOPrice.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sirus-addons/Auctionator/HEAD/Auctionator/Source/Selling/LIFOPrice.lua)
- A companion addon (PreserveAuctionatorAHScan) is described as caching "the raw scan data from the last Auctionator AH scan". [tool docs] — [GitHub API listing: Auctionator/PreserveAuctionatorAHScan](https://api.github.com/users/Auctionator/repos?per_page=50)

### Inferences
- "Price" in the Auctionator ecosystem means "lowest listing in the most recent scan", with no outlier protection. Any addon that wants to be a drop-in price source for CraftSim-style consumers only needs to answer "min buyout by item ID / item link" plus an optional vendor price.
- Storing three numbers per item per day (low of lows, high of lows, max quantity) is enough to draw a price-history list and is a workable minimum for a scan-only addon's saved variables.

### Gaps
- Current upstream Auctionator source could not be opened (repository 404), so the present-day API surface (age, exact-match, disenchant value, DB-update callbacks, shopping-list creation) and the current crafting-info formula are not verified. Only the functions listed above are confirmed.
- Why the upstream repository is no longer public was not found.
- The crafting-info profit formula (for example whether the AH cut is subtracted) is not verified from code.

## 3. Other addons

### Takeaway
The remaining addons each cover one niche: Auctioneer is the only one with a family of selectable statistics modules; aux and RECrystallize show two minimal scan-only price models (weighted median of daily minimums vs. plain last-seen minimum); CraftSim, LootAppraiser and Journalator are consumers of someone else's prices; BagSync and Altoholic are inventory trackers.

### Cited Findings

**Auctioneer (Retail up to 11.0.2 beta, Classic, Classic TBC; last CurseForge update 2024-09-29, so no 12.x build was found)**
- Modules: Appraiser (posting with remembered prices, batch posting, "Match Competition" undercut within limits), CompactUI, SearchUI (searchers for Resale, Disenchant, Prospecting and Vendor deals; RealTimeSearch rechecks first and last AH pages), BeanCounter (logs bids, postings and mail for overall and per-item profit), Enchantrix (disenchant / mill / prospect values), Informant (vendor prices and item uses). About 33.5M downloads. [tool docs] — [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer)
- Statistic modules as described by the authors: Market (a "custom designed formula that merges the various statistics into a usable price"), Histogram (median and IQR over all prices ever seen), iLevel (groups items by rarity, type and item level), Purchased (infers purchase prices from items that disappear before they could have expired; needs frequent scanning), Sales (uses BeanCounter's real buy/sell history), Simple (averages plus exponential moving averages over 3, 7 and 14 days), StdDev (keeps the last 100 price points and excludes outliers for a normalized mean). [tool docs] — [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer)

**aux (Classic 1.13 client; repository last pushed 2026-01-19)**
- Features: full replacement AH UI, automatic multi-page scans, saved and recent searches, advanced filters with logical operators, sorting by percentage of historical value, "real time mode" that continuously scans the newest auctions, posting with one-click undercut and per-item saved settings, tooltips with historical value, vendor prices and disenchant value, `/aux scan` full scan used for price history. [tool docs] — [aux-addon README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shirsig/aux-addon/master/README.md)
- History algorithm: during a day it tracks the minimum unit buyout seen (`daily_min_buyout`); at local midnight that value is pushed as a data point and at most 11 data points are kept. The "value" of an item is the weighted median of those daily minimums, with weight `0.99 ^ days_before_newest_point`, normalized; if there are no data points yet it falls back to today's minimum. [source code] — [aux-addon core/history.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shirsig/aux-addon/master/core/history.lua)

**RECrystallize (Retail; TOC `Interface: 120005`, version 2.1.2; repository last pushed 2026-04-22)**
- Scans with `C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems()`, reads every row, and records unit price = price / count per item variant; commodity stacks smaller than a configurable size are ignored; only items of quality above poor or with transmog info are stored. [source code] — [RECrystallize.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize/HEAD/RECrystallize.lua)
- Storage: per item ID and variant string, `{Price = lowest unit price in the scan, LastSeen = scan time}`; commodities go to a region-wide table, other items to a realm table; records not seen for longer than a configurable cleanup period are deleted. [source code] — [RECrystallize.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize/HEAD/RECrystallize.lua)
- Public API: `RECrystallize_PriceCheck(link)` returns price, lastSeen and a `partial` flag (true when the exact variant was missing and the cheapest variant was used); `RECrystallize_PriceCheckItemID(itemID)` returns the cheapest variant's price and lastSeen. [source code] — [RECrystallize.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize/HEAD/RECrystallize.lua)

**CraftSim (Retail only on CurseForge; file 27.0.7 on 2026-09-28; repository pushed 2026-11-19; about 10.5M downloads)**
- Features: profession stat weights and average profit per craft, profit-optimal profession gear, reagent quality optimization, concentration profit per point, price details per outcome quality, simulation mode, price overrides per material, recipe scan of a whole profession, craft queue (including patron work orders), customer history, export of the queue as an Auctionator shopping list, craft logging against predictions. Price sources: TSM, Auctionator, RECrystallize, Oribos Exchange. [tool docs] — [CurseForge: CraftSim](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftsim)
- Price lookup order in code: vendor price first when the source provides one, then the source's minimum buyout; for OribosExchange it uses the realm `market` value and falls back to the `region` value. With TSM the price strings for reagents and for crafted items are separately configurable, and TSM is also used for an expected deposit and a "smart restock" amount. [source code] — [CraftSim DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/derfloh205/CraftSim/main/DataSource/PriceAPIs.lua)

**Journalator (repository pushed 2026-10-19; README names Retail, SoM and Wrath)**
- Tracks AH sales, postings, expired and cancelled auctions, WoW Token purchases (Retail), vendor buys and sells, repairs, flight costs, crafting orders (Retail), trading post, quest rewards, loot, mail and trades; offers a summary view, monthly sales statistics and spreadsheet export; individual monitors can be switched off. [tool docs] — [GitHub: Journalator](https://github.com/Auctionator/Journalator)

**OribosExchange (Retail data addon from Undermine Exchange) and a Forever fork**
- API: `OEMarketInfo(item, table)` fills `market` (median market price on this AH over the past 4 days), `region` (median market price across the region's realms), `days` (days since last seen, 0-250; 251 = seen more than 250 days ago; 252 = sold by vendors), `age` (seconds since the data was compiled); prices are in copper but precise only to the silver. `OETooltip(bool)` toggles its tooltip lines. `TUJMarketInfo` is kept as an alias. [source code] — [shatari addon/OribosExchange.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/addon/OribosExchange.lua)
- The Retail addon data is regenerated and repackaged 3 times a week. [tool docs] — [shatari README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/README.md)
- A third-party fork ships hourly data for WoW Forever (interface 16001) and defines its lines as: AH line = 4-day median of the daily lowest listings; region line = median across the region's auction houses. It adds crafting cost in the profession window (reagent cost at AH prices, item value after the 5% cut, profit; profit is omitted when a reagent has no listing) and a data-age line that turns red after three days. [tool docs, third-party fork] — [OribosExchange-Forever README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZiiMs/OribosExchange-Forever/HEAD/README.md)

**Point Blank Sniper**
- Repository description: "Limited support and updates. Find great deals using a shopping list."; last pushed 2026-10-14. [tool docs] — [GitHub: PointBlankSniper](https://github.com/TheMouseNest/PointBlankSniper)

**LootAppraiser (Reloaded)**
- Repository description: "An addon which keeps track of looted items and relative AH value"; last pushed 2026-11-14. [tool docs] — [GitHub: LootAppraiserReloaded](https://github.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded)

**Inventory tools**
- BagSync: tracks bags, equipped items, bank, reagent bank, guild bank, void storage, mailbox, currencies and auction listings across characters, shows per-character counts in tooltips, has a cross-character search with query syntax, and records professions and gold. Flavors: Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Titan Reforged Classic, Classic; last update 2026-11-17; about 5.0M downloads. [tool docs] — [CurseForge: BagSync](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/bagsync)
- Altoholic has separate repositories per client (Retail, Cata, Vanilla); the Retail one was last pushed 2026-10-14. [primary data] — [GitHub API search: Altoholic](https://api.github.com/search/repositories?q=Altoholic+in:name&sort=stars&per_page=4)

**Saddlebag Exchange addon**
- The addon produces JSON of the player's own auctions (item or pet ID, price, auctionID per realm) after opening the Auctions tab; the player pastes it into the website or Discord bot, which compares it hourly with Blizzard API data and alerts on undercuts or when the listing is gone ("probably sold"). Only non-commodities are covered because commodities are "almost always undercut within an hour". [tool docs] — [Saddlebag wiki: WoW Discord Undercut Alerts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ff14-advanced-market-search/saddlebag-with-pockets/master/app/content/blog/wiki/wow-discord-undercut-alerts.md)

**Popularity signal**
- CurseForge download totals at the time of research: Auctionator about 215.4M, TSM about 64.6M, Auctioneer about 33.5M, CraftSim about 10.5M, BagSync about 5.0M. [primary data] — [Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator), [TSM](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tradeskill-master), [Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer), [CraftSim](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/craftsim), [BagSync](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/bagsync)

### Inferences
- Download counts suggest players value a fast, simple buy/sell/cancel UI (Auctionator) far more widely than deep configuration (TSM); download totals are cumulative and include auto-updates, so this is a weak signal.
- A scan-only addon has three proven "value" models to choose from: last-seen minimum (Auctionator, RECrystallize), weighted median of daily minimums (aux, OribosExchange), and trimmed mean of the cheap end plus a decayed daily average (TSM). The median-of-daily-minimum family is the cheapest to store.
- Exposing `GetMinBuyoutByItemID / ByItemLink`-style functions is what makes an addon usable as a price source by crafting tools; CraftSim's adapter shows the minimum contract.
- Auctioneer's "Purchased" module is prior art for estimating sales from successive scans (see section 5).

### Gaps
- Auctioneer's module source was not opened; the statistics descriptions are the authors' CurseForge text, and the formula of its default "Market" price is not documented there.
- README files for Point Blank Sniper and LootAppraiserReloaded could not be opened; how PBS thresholds deals and which price sources LootAppraiser supports are unverified.
- Journalator's current client list may be newer than its README states; its profit calculation is not documented in the README.
- Altoholic's feature list was not opened.
- No r/woweconomy or other community comparison threads were consulted, so "what players value most" rests on tool descriptions and download counts only.

## 4. Websites and the Blizzard Game Data API

### Takeaway
Every website is a consumer of Blizzard's hourly auction snapshots (per connected realm, plus one regional commodities document). They differ in what they add: Undermine Exchange stores cheapest price and total quantity per snapshot; Saddlebag Exchange layers TSM's sale-rate and average-price data on top of current minimum prices to rank markets, deals and shortages.

### Cited Findings

**Blizzard Game Data API**
- Endpoint `/data/wow/connected-realm/{id}/auctions` in namespace `dynamic-{region}` was announced on 2020-02-12; the update interval "was initially set at 1 hour; however, it might change over time without notice"; responses can exceed 10 MB. Every Game Data and Profile endpoint returns `Last-Modified` and supports `If-Modified-Since`. Posted by forum user Araspir in the API Discussion category; the opened page did not label the poster's role. [official] — [Blizzard forums: World of Warcraft API Update - Visions of N'zoth](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/blizzard/t/world-of-warcraft-api-update-visions-of-nzoth/3461)
- With patch 9.2.7 (post dated 2022-08-18) commodities moved to a regional endpoint `/wow/auctions/commodities`, "updating every hour (same cadence as the other auction data)"; the quota cost of that document was raised to 25 (from 1), and `if-modified-since` requests also incur the full cost. Posted by Korakk, speaking as "we"; role not labelled on the opened page. [official] — [Blizzard forums: Immediate change to Auction APIs for Commodities with 9.2.7](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/blizzard/t/immediate-change-to-auction-apis-for-commodities-with-927/31522)
- Per-auction fields used by a production consumer: `id`, `quantity`, and one of `unit_price`, `buyout` or `bid`, plus `item` with `bonus_lists` and `modifiers`. [source code] — [shatari src/realmProcess.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/realmProcess.js)

**Undermine Exchange (Retail and Forever; active, backend repository pushed 2026-10-17)**
- It provides hourly-resolution pricing for the past 14 days and daily-resolution pricing since September 2022, for about 186 realms plus 4 region-wide commodity "realms" and over 30,000 items; it is a static site whose backend rewrites data files as hourly snapshots arrive. It was renamed from Oribos Exchange. [tool docs] — [shatari README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/README.md), [shatari-front README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari-front/HEAD/README.md)
- Per snapshot and item the backend keeps `p` = the lowest price among the item's auctions and `q` = total quantity, plus a price-to-quantity map of the current listings; equipment and pets are also tracked per variation. Item state holds a list of `[snapshot time, price, quantity]` for 14 days (`MAX_HISTORY`) and a daily list in which today's entry is replaced whenever the new snapshot's quantity is at least the stored one. [source code] — [shatari src/realmProcess.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/realmProcess.js), [shatari src/constants.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/constants.js)
- Region state stores a median per item, and a "deals" state stores a `[median, dealPrice]` pair per item. The backend code defines products `mainline` and `forever`. [source code] — [shatari src/dealState.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/dealState.js), [shatari src/constants.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/constants.js)
- The site lists realm selection, category browsing, name search, a result list showing when unavailable items were last seen, a detail view with current listings, pricing statistics, charts and heat maps, and patron-only options (prices by item level, pet breeds, region median column, Arbitrage Mode, tertiary stat search) for $3.00 per month; it says it was created for players of World of Warcraft: Forever alongside Midnight. [tool docs] — [undermine.exchange](https://undermine.exchange/)

**The Undermine Journal (historic; source last pushed 2023-02-27)**
- Architecture: one fetcher per region polls the auction API, a parser saves stats per snapshot, hourly detailed history is pruned after 2 weeks, a daily table powers OHLC charts for stackable items, a job maintains global averages, and an expensive job builds the in-game addon data. [tool docs] — [newsstand README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/newsstand/master/README.md)
- Definitions by its author: Market Price = the cost per item to buy the cheapest 15% of the item's total available quantity at one moment on one realm (the last auction counted partially); Mean = average over time; Median = midpoint of the sorted market prices over time; a "global price" was the average of US and EU regional medians observed 8 times over 24 hours. [tool docs] — [Erorus gist: method.md](https://gist.github.com/erorus/09e2d19f1dd114c3588ff4141063a760)
- Addon data build: per realm item it emitted an average of stored hourly prices, a "recent" average restricted to the last 72 hours, the standard deviation of the hourly prices, and days since last seen (capped at 251; 252 = vendor item); global median, mean and standard deviation came from a separate table. [source code] — [newsstand scripts/buildaddon.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/newsstand/master/scripts/buildaddon.php)
- Global stats job: for each item it computes mean, population standard deviation and median across prices, then averages those rows per item, level and region. [source code] — [newsstand scripts/itemglobal.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/newsstand/master/scripts/itemglobal.php)

**Saddlebag Exchange (Retail; some Forever and Classic support; frontend repository pushed 2026-11-19)**
- WoW tools listed on the site: Best Deals, Azeroth Auction Assassin sniper (external desktop app), Weekly Price Group Delta, Upload Timers ("the time each hour when the Blizzard API AH data updates"), Shopping List (realms with the lowest price for an item), Item Export Search (best servers to sell on), Raid BOE item-level Shopping List and Export Search, Ultra Rare, Current Content / Legacy / Pet Marketshare Overviews, Region Wide Undercut Checker with its CurseForge addon, Server Transfer Trading Search, Commodity Shortage Futures, Out of Stock, Big Goblin Tracker ("items controlled by big goblins who are oligopolists"), Local Realm Shortage Finder, Commodity Shortage Finder, Price Sniper and Price Spike Alerts, curated flipping lists; the navigation also has a WoW Forever Upload Timers page. [tool docs] — [saddlebagexchange.com/wow](https://saddlebagexchange.com/wow)

[tool docs] — [saddlebagexchange.com Best Deals](https://saddlebagexchange.com/wow/best-deals/recommended)

[tool docs] — [Saddlebag wiki: price group deltas](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ff14-advanced-market-search/saddlebag-with-pockets/master/app/content/blog/wiki/how-to-use-price-group-deltas.md)

[tool docs] — [wowauctions.net](https://www.wowauctions.net/)

### Inferences
- "Minimum price now ÷ long-run average" with fixed bands (±15%, ±60%) and "current quantity ÷ average quantity" are simple signals that a scan-only addon can compute locally from its own history; Saddlebag's dependence on TSM for sold-per-day is the part that cannot be copied directly.
- Undermine Exchange's daily point is effectively the day's snapshot with the highest quantity, a pragmatic way to pick one representative snapshot per day without averaging.
- The hourly cadence of the public API bounds what websites can know; an in-game scanner can sample more or less often but is limited by the client throttles in section 6.

## 5. Statistical ideas across tools, and estimating sales from snapshots alone

### Takeaway
All tools are variations on a few robust-statistics moves: look only at the cheap end of the order book, cut at price jumps, prefer medians or trimmed means, and smooth over days with decaying weights. None of the opened sources documents a validated method for inferring sales from snapshots; the practical approach in the wild is "a listing that vanished before it could have expired was probably sold", which needs frequent scans and conflates sales with cancellations.

### Cited Findings

**Ideas as implemented by tools**
- Cheapest-fraction pricing: The Undermine Journal priced the cheapest 15% of available quantity; TSM considers at most the cheapest 30% of auctions. [tool docs] — [Erorus gist](https://gist.github.com/erorus/09e2d19f1dd114c3588ff4141063a760), [TSM market value article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- Gap detection: TSM stops at the first 20% jump between consecutive prices once 15% of auctions are included. [tool docs] — [TSM market value article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated)
- Standard-deviation trimming: TSM drops prices beyond 1.5 standard deviations before averaging; Auctioneer's StdDev module keeps the last 100 points and excludes outliers. [tool docs] — [TSM market value article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated), [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer)
- Medians: aux uses a weighted median of up to 11 daily minimum buyouts; OribosExchange exposes a 4-day median for the realm and a median across realms for the region; Auctioneer's Histogram module reports median and IQR. [source code] — [aux history.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shirsig/aux-addon/master/core/history.lua), [OribosExchange.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/addon/OribosExchange.lua); [tool docs] — [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer)
- Decayed averages: TSM uses a 14-day weighted average with a half-life a little over 2 days; Auctioneer's Simple module tracks exponential moving averages over 3, 7 and 14 days; aux decays weights by 0.99 per day. [tool docs] — [TSM market value article](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/en_US/auctiondb-market-value/how-is-auctiondb-market-value-calculated), [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer); [source code] — [aux history.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shirsig/aux-addon/master/core/history.lua)
- Long and short windows side by side: TSM's DBMarket (14 days) vs DBHistorical (60 days) vs DBRecent (latest snapshot); The Undermine Journal's overall average vs 72-hour "recent" average with a standard deviation. [tool docs] — [TSM price sources](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean); [source code] — [newsstand buildaddon.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/newsstand/master/scripts/buildaddon.php)
- Percent-of-reference bands: Saddlebag classifies markets by current minimum ÷ average with ±15% and ±60% thresholds and tracks current ÷ average quantity. [tool docs] — [Saddlebag market overview guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ff14-advanced-market-search/saddlebag-with-pockets/master/app/content/blog/wiki/how-to-trade-using-our-wow-market-overview.md)
- Staleness as a first-class value: OribosExchange returns days since last seen and data age; RECrystallize returns LastSeen with every price. [source code] — [OribosExchange.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/addon/OribosExchange.lua), [RECrystallize.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize/HEAD/RECrystallize.lua)

**Reference statistics**
- Z-score is `(Y_i - mean) / s`; the modified z-score is `0.6745 * (x_i - median) / MAD`, with absolute values above 3.5 labelled potential outliers (Iglewicz and Hoaglin); plain z-scores "can be misleading (particularly for small sample sizes)" because the largest possible z-score is bounded by `(n - 1) / sqrt(n)`. [official] — [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, 1.3.5.17 Detection of Outliers](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda35h.htm)
- Single exponential smoothing: `S_t = α·y_(t-1) + (1 - α)·S_(t-1)`, 0 < α ≤ 1; the weight on an observation k periods back is `α(1 - α)^k`, decreasing geometrically and summing to one; α is usually chosen to minimize mean squared error. [official] — [NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook, 6.4.3.1 Single Exponential Smoothing](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section4/pmc431.htm)

**Sale rate and sold quantity**
- TSM defines region sale rate as the share of postings that sell and sold-per-day as average volume sold per auction house per day, and states that its regional sale average is built from users' Accounting data uploaded through the desktop app, i.e. from observed transactions, not from listing snapshots. [tool docs] — [TSM value sources](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/custom-strings/which-value-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean), [TSM price sources](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- TSM's personal equivalents are computed from the player's own history: SaleRate (share of your postings that sold) and NumExpires (expires or cancels since the last sale). [tool docs] — [TSM value sources](https://tradeskillmaster.helpjuice.com/custom-strings/which-value-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean)
- Snapshot-based inference in existing tools: Auctioneer's Purchased statistic "infers purchase prices from items that sell before they could expire" and requires frequent scanning; Saddlebag treats one of your listings disappearing from the next hourly snapshot as "probably sold". [tool docs] — [CurseForge: Auctioneer](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctioneer), [Saddlebag undercut alerts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ff14-advanced-market-search/saddlebag-with-pockets/master/app/content/blog/wiki/wow-discord-undercut-alerts.md)
- The public API gives each auction a stable `id`; a production backend builds, per item, a key string of `id-quantity` pairs for the current snapshot and also loads the previous snapshot's map (what it does with the difference was not read). [source code] — [shatari src/realmProcess.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/realmProcess.js)
- Auction durations bound when a listing can expire: 12 / 24 / 48 hours on the modern API (`PostItem` duration 1 / 2 / 3) and 2 / 8 / 24 hours on the legacy Classic API (`PostAuction` runTime 1 / 2 / 3). [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.PostItem](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.PostItem), [PostAuction](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_PostAuction)
- The client exposes remaining time only as bands; TSM documents them as Short (under 30 minutes), Medium (30 minutes to 2 hours), Long (2 to 12 hours). The replicate API has `GetReplicateItemTimeLeft`. [tool docs] — [TSM Auctioning operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/tsm-addon-documentation/1072146-tsm-addon-auctioning-operations); [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems)

**Academic literature located**
- Conlon, C. T. and Mortimer, J. H. (2008, revised 2011), "Demand Estimation Under Incomplete Product Availability", NBER Working Paper 14315, DOI 10.3386/w14315; published in American Economic Journal: Microeconomics 5(4), 1-30 (2013). It develops a procedure that allows for changes in product availability "even when availability is only observed periodically", using vending machines whose inventory was observed every four hours, and argues that ignoring availability changes biases demand estimates. [working paper; published version peer-reviewed] — [NBER w14315](https://www.nber.org/papers/w14315)
- Morrison, M. and Fontenla, M. (2013), "Price convergence in an online virtual world", Empirical Economics 44(3), 1053-1064, DOI 10.1007/s00181-012-0567-3. Using World of Warcraft data from eight structurally identical servers, it finds price convergence in all eight with the Phillips and Sul (2007) test and a "band of inaction" test. [peer-reviewed] — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:spr:empeco:v:44:y:2013:i:3:p:1053-1064)

### Inferences
- A scan-only estimator of sold quantity between two scans, assembled from the cited pieces: for each item, match listings across scans (by price, stack size and time-left band, since an in-game scan may not expose the API's auction id); count listings that disappeared while their previous time-left band was longer than the gap between scans as "sold or cancelled"; treat disappearances from the Short band as ambiguous. This is the Auctioneer "Purchased" idea made explicit. It is an inference, not a documented algorithm.
- Biases to expect: cancel-and-repost (undercut wars) looks like sales, so the estimate is an upper bound; scan gaps longer than the shortest duration band lose information; for commodities bought cheapest-first, disappearance of quantity at the bottom of the price ladder is more likely a sale than disappearance in the middle. The Conlon and Mortimer result supports treating periodically observed stock as censored, not as exact sales.
- Sale rate in the TSM sense (sales ÷ postings) can only be measured exactly for the player's own auctions (mail and owned-auction events). For the market, a proxy is estimated sold quantity ÷ (estimated sold quantity + listings that reached the Short band and then vanished).
- With small numbers of listings, mean ± k·standard deviation is weak (the z-score bound above); a median/MAD rule or the price-jump rule behaves better for thin items, which matches why aux and OribosExchange use medians.
- A 60%/15% band on "current minimum ÷ own long-run value" and "current ÷ average quantity" give deal and shortage flags without any external feed.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed or working paper was found that estimates sales from auction-house or order-book listing snapshots in a game economy; the two papers above are adjacent (periodic inventory observation; WoW price convergence), not direct methods.
- Leys, Ley, Klein, Bernard and Licata (2013), "Detecting outliers: Do not use standard deviation around the mean, use absolute deviation around the median", Journal of Experimental Social Psychology 49(4), 764-766, DOI 10.1016/j.jesp.2013.03.013, is the usual citation for preferring MAD over standard deviation, but the publisher page could not be opened, so its content is not verified here. Huber (1964), "Robust Estimation of a Location Parameter", Annals of Mathematical Statistics 35(1), DOI 10.1214/aoms/1177703732, likewise could not be opened.
- Whether the in-game replicate or browse results expose a stable auction identifier for other players' listings was not verified; the matching approach above assumes they may not.
- How accurate disappearance-based sale estimates are in practice (versus TSM's transaction-based numbers) is not measured in any source opened.
- Auctioneer's "Purchased" implementation details (thresholds, how it treats cancellations) were not read from source.

## 6. Staying within Blizzard's rules: hardware events, throttles, policy

### Takeaway
Reading the auction house is throttled (a full "replicate"/getAll dump once per 15 minutes per account, ordinary queries through a throttled message system), while every money-moving action (post, bid/buyout, cancel, start a commodity purchase) needs a hardware event and has been blocked from macro or script execution since patch 9.1.5. Addons comply by doing analysis automatically and binding each action to one user click.

### Cited Findings

**Modern API (Retail, Forever, Mists Classic)**
- `C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems()` queries all auctions; there is a 15-minute account-wide throttle between successful calls; results are read with `GetNumReplicateItems` and `GetReplicateItemInfo(index)`; `REPLICATE_ITEM_LIST_UPDATE` fires after a successful call and for each item cached later. Added in 8.3.0 replacing `QueryAuctionItems` getAll. On servers with more than 80k auctions it disconnected clients; a 9.0.2 hotfix made `owner` return nil for other players' auctions; roughly 2000 update events fire per frame; items may be uncached and need `Item:ContinueOnItemLoad`. Listed for game types standard, forever and mists. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.ReplicateItems)
- `C_AuctionHouse.SendBrowseQuery(query)` (added 8.3.0, replacing `QueryAuctionItems`) is restricted and cannot be called from `/run`, `/script` or `loadstring`; the page warns doing so "will brick the AH". Related throttle events: `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_MESSAGE_QUEUED / SENT / DROPPED / RESPONSE_RECEIVED` and `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY`; `C_AuctionHouse.IsThrottledMessageSystemReady` exists. Listed for standard, forever, mists. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.SendBrowseQuery](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.SendBrowseQuery), [Patch 8.3.0/API changes](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_8.3.0/API_changes)
- `C_AuctionHouse.PostItem(item, duration, quantity [, bid [, buyout]])`: requires a hardware event (`#hwevent`) and is `#noscript`; duration 1/2/3 = 12/24/48 h; prices must be whole silver (non-zero copper fails silently); became protected from macro or script calls in 9.1.5 (2021-11-02); returns `needsConfirmation` since 9.2.5. Listed for standard 12.1.5, forever 1.60.1, mists 5.5.4. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.PostItem](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.PostItem)
- `C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid(auctionID, bidAmount)`: `#hwevent`, `#noscript`; non-commodity items only; amount must be whole silver; protected from macro or script calls since 9.1.5. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid)
- `C_AuctionHouse.StartCommoditiesPurchase(itemID, quantity)`: `#hwevent`, `#noscript`, protected since 9.1.5; the purchase is completed with `ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase(itemID, quantity)` using the same arguments, after a short delay or on `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY`; the Confirm page itself lists no hardware-event tag. [reference wiki] — [StartCommoditiesPurchase](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.StartCommoditiesPurchase), [ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase)
- `C_AuctionHouse.CancelAuction(ownedAuctionID)`: restricted, protected from macro or script calls since 9.1.5. The API index tags `CancelAuction`, `PlaceBid`, `PostCommodity`, `PostItem` and `StartCommoditiesPurchase` with `#hwevent` and `#noscript`. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.CancelAuction](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.CancelAuction), [World of Warcraft API index](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/World_of_Warcraft_API)

**Legacy API (Vanilla / TBC / Mists Classic clients)**
- `QueryAuctionItems(text, minLevel, maxLevel, page, usable, rarity, getAll, exactMatch, filterData)`: normal queries "appear to be throttled at 0.3 seconds", getAll mode is throttled to 15 minutes, and `CanSendAuctionQuery()` reports when each mode is allowed; getAll was added in 2.3.0, may disconnect low-bandwidth players, and returned at most 42,554 items as of 4.0.1; search text over 63 bytes may disconnect; no effect when the AH window is closed. Removed from Retail in 8.3.0; still listed for mists, tbc and vanilla (not for forever). [reference wiki] — [QueryAuctionItems](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_QueryAuctionItems)
- `PostAuction(minBid, buyoutPrice, runTime, stackSize, numStacks, warningAcknowledged)`: `#hwevent` and `#noscript`; runTime 1/2/3 = 2/8/24 h; added in 1.13.2 replacing `StartAuction`; `warningAcknowledged` added in 1.15.7 (2025-04-08). [reference wiki] — [PostAuction](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_PostAuction)
- `PlaceAuctionBid(type, index, bid)` "can only be successfully called after a hardware event ... since patch 2.0"; removed from Retail in 8.3.0, present in the Classic clients. [reference wiki] — [PlaceAuctionBid](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_PlaceAuctionBid)

**Crafting and mail**
- `C_TradeSkillUI.CraftRecipe(recipeSpellID [, numCasts [, craftingReagents [, itemTarget [, applyConcentration]]]])`: the page lists no hardware-event tag (only `AllowedWhenUntainted`); listed for standard and forever. [reference wiki] — [C_TradeSkillUI.CraftRecipe](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_TradeSkillUI.CraftRecipe)
- `SendMail(recipient, subject [, body])`: `#noscript`, protected from macro script calls since 9.1.5; repeated calls are ignored until `MAIL_SEND_SUCCESS` or `MAIL_FAILED` fires. Listed for standard, forever, mists, tbc, vanilla. [reference wiki] — [SendMail](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_SendMail)

**How tools adapt**
- TSM's Retail sniper is a repeated search with a 30-second delay between searches; its Classic sniper rescans the last page. [tool docs] — [TSM Sniper Operations](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/tsm-addon-documentation/tsm-addon-sniper-operations)
- RECrystallize starts its full scan from its auction-house button handler, disables the button while waiting, and prints a warning every 60 seconds if the server is slow to deliver the replicate data. [source code] — [RECrystallize.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AcidWeb/RECrystallize/HEAD/RECrystallize.lua)
- External snipers avoid the client entirely by polling the web API and alerting the player, who then buys by hand. [tool docs] — [AzerothAuctionAssassin README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ff14-advanced-market-search/AzerothAuctionAssassin/main/README.md)

**Policy**
- Blizzard's UI Add-On Development Policy (public since 2009-03-20, thread posted by Kaivax): add-ons must be free of charge, code must not be hidden or obfuscated, add-ons must not negatively impact realms or other players (for example excessive chat use), may not include advertisements or solicit donations in game, must respect the game's rating and the Terms of Use, and Blizzard may disable add-on functionality at its discretion. The policy text does not mention automation directly. [official] — [Blizzard forums: UI Add-On Development Policy](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ui-add-on-development-policy/24534)

### Inferences
- The compliant pattern for a scan-only helper: one user click starts a replicate scan (at most once per 15 minutes per account); all statistics run offline from saved data; every post, buy, bid and cancel is one user click on a button that calls the restricted function directly from the click handler; commodity purchases need the click on Start and can confirm after the throttle-ready event.
- Because the replicate throttle is account-wide, per-item browse/search queries are the only way to refresh specific prices between full scans, and they are rate-limited by the throttled message system, so refresh lists should be short and queued on `AUCTION_HOUSE_THROTTLED_SYSTEM_READY`.
- Forever exposes the modern `C_AuctionHouse` namespace (the wiki lists forever on ReplicateItems, SendBrowseQuery, PostItem, PlaceBid, commodity purchase and CancelAuction pages and does not list forever on `QueryAuctionItems`), so Classic-era getAll techniques do not apply there.

### Gaps
- The wiki pages do not state numeric limits for the retail throttled message system (queries per second), and no official Blizzard statement of those numbers was found.
- Whether `C_TradeSkillUI.CraftRecipe` needs a hardware event in practice is not stated on its page; treat as unverified.
- No official Blizzard post was opened that explains the reasoning behind the 9.1.5 protection change or the 15-minute replicate limit; dates and behavior come from the reference wiki.
- A classic "full-scan query cooldown" other than the 15-minute getAll throttle and the roughly 0.3-second page-query throttle was not found.

## 7. "WoW Forever", interface 16001, game type "camelot"

### Takeaway
There is public information: the reference wiki documents World of Warcraft: Forever as a released Blizzard client (launch 2026-11-04) with interface version 16001, internal name Camelot, and TOC game types `forever` / `camelot`, and the major auction addons and price sites already list Forever support.

### Cited Findings
- World of Warcraft: Forever is described as a Blizzard MMORPG for Windows and macOS, "an alternative version that retains the core mechanics and aesthetic of the game's original 2004 release", announced at BlizzCon 2026 and released on 2026-11-04, with a level cap of 60; community nickname "Classic+", internal development name "Camelot". [reference wiki] — [warcraft.wiki.gg: World of Warcraft: Forever (Patch 1.60)](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.60)
- The TOC format page lists interface `16001` for Forever (Midnight standard 120105, Mists 50504, Vanilla 11509), TOC suffixes `AddOnName_Forever.toc` and `AddOnName_Camelot.toc`, and `AllowLoadGameType` values `forever` and `camelot`; it notes "The Camelot value may be changed to Forever in the future" and that patch 1.60.1 "Added Camelot game type, Game variable value and TOC suffix, with Forever game type and TOC suffix alias". The family suffix `_Mainline.toc` is listed as covering standard, forever, camelot, plunderstorm and wowhack, and the Midnight-specific suffix is `_Standard.toc`. [reference wiki] — [warcraft.wiki.gg: TOC format](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/TOC_format)
- API pages list Forever as build 1.60.1 (69913) with the modern auction API available. [reference wiki] — [C_AuctionHouse.PostItem](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_AuctionHouse.PostItem)
- Tools already listing Forever: TSM and Auctionator and BagSync on CurseForge; wowauctions.net (US and EU); Undermine Exchange (site text and a `forever` product in its backend); Saddlebag Exchange (a Forever upload-timers page). [tool docs] — [CurseForge: TSM](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tradeskill-master), [CurseForge: Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator), [CurseForge: BagSync](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/bagsync), [wowauctions.net](https://www.wowauctions.net/), [undermine.exchange](https://undermine.exchange/), [saddlebagexchange.com/wow](https://saddlebagexchange.com/wow); [source code] — [shatari constants.js](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/erorus/shatari/HEAD/src/constants.js)
- A third-party OribosExchange port for Forever states that Blizzard publishes Forever auction data through its API for US/EU/TW/KR with 12/12/6/6 auction houses, and that Forever has separate auction houses per ruleset (PvP, Normal, Roleplay, Roleplay PvP) and faction (Alliance, Horde, Neutral). [tool docs, third-party fork] — [OribosExchange-Forever README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ZiiMs/OribosExchange-Forever/HEAD/README.md)

### Inferences
- Per the wiki's suffix table, a `_Mainline.toc` file is a family fallback that also matches Forever clients, while `_Standard.toc` is the Midnight-only suffix; an addon that wants different file lists for Midnight and Forever should rely on the specific `_Forever` / `_Camelot` / `_Standard` suffixes or `AllowLoadGameType`, not on `_Mainline` meaning "retail only".
- Faction- and ruleset-specific auction houses on Forever mean scan data should be keyed by realm ruleset and faction, not only by realm.

### Gaps
- No official Blizzard page (patch notes or developer documentation) for Forever's interface number or game type was opened; the facts above come from the reference wiki and tool listings.
- The Forever auction API endpoint path and namespace were not verified from Blizzard documentation; the port's README is the only source for the number of auction houses.
- Whether the 15-minute replicate throttle and commodity (region-wide) market behave identically on Forever was not verified beyond the wiki listing those functions for the forever game type.
