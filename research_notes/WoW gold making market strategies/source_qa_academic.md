# Source audit: market_theory.md, price_dynamics.md, trading_strategies.md

Audit date: 2026-11-20. Scope: every source tagged [peer-reviewed], [working paper], [textbook], [official] or [primary data] (52 after de-duplication), plus a spot-check of 28 [reference wiki] / [tool docs] / [community] sources that carry addon-relevant claims (fees, automation rules, WoW Forever, numeric trading thresholds).

Method: bibliographic records checked against Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works/<DOI>`), arXiv API, RePEc, NBER, publisher pages. Full texts were read where openly available (PDF converted to text locally, or ar5iv HTML). Evidence is given as figures and locators (table, section, line) rather than verbatim quotation; "phrase present" means the wording quoted in the notes was found in the source.

Verdict key: VERIFIED (full text read, supports claim) / VERIFIED-ABSTRACT / PARTLY / UNSUPPORTED / NOT FOUND / UNREACHABLE.

### Kosminsky (2010) figures checked against the full text

- Data: wowecon.com, 13 Sep-13 Oct 2008, at least 1,000 auctions per server; census from WarcraftRealms; essence data game-wide from Allakhazam.
- Median / mean / SD by server (Tables 1.1-1.6): 12.00 / 12.29 / 1.50; 16.25 / 16.21 / 1.73; 12.00 / 11.97 / 2.09; 14.33 / 14.30 / 2.61; 11.77 / 11.49 / 0.92; 17.50 / 17.48 / 0.87.
- Average SD: low 1.62, medium 2.35, high 0.895; average F 3.76, p < .001. The 12.9% figure is the average difference in standard deviation between the two servers of a pair, not a price difference.
- Level share 58-70 vs median price: in each pair the higher-share server has the higher median (57.9 vs 76.6%, 63.6 vs 71.2%, 57.5 vs 79.7%); quadratic fit R² .993 on six points.
- Essences (Table 3): mode 5.00 / 15.00 (0%); mean 6.02 / 17.12 vs predicted 5.71 / 18.06 (-5.15% / +5.49%); about 5% profit from splitting Greater into Lesser.
- Acknowledgement names the Byram Hills Science Research Department; author affiliation printed as MIT.

## Safe to rely on

- Robust statistics for outlier-prone data: MAD definition, modified Z-score with 0.6745 and the 3.5 cutoff, Z-score ceiling (n-1)/sqrt(n) (NIST, verified).
- EWMA and CUSUM formulas and parameter conventions for detecting sustained level shifts (NIST, verified).
- Classical decomposition steps and its stated limits; simple exponential smoothing (Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, verified).
- Arbitrage as negative-cycle detection on negated log rates; DAG shortest/longest paths in E + V (Sedgewick & Wayne, verified).
- Kelly growth-optimal fraction for an even-money bet and the ruin result for betting everything (Kelly 1956, verified in full text).
- Inventory-aware reservation price and exponential fill-rate model (Avellaneda & Stoikov, verified in full text; published in Quantitative Finance 2008).
- Square-root price impact with its validated range of small Q/V in futures markets (Tóth et al., verified in full text). It is not evidence about auction houses.
- Kosminsky (2010) numbers for one item on six servers over 30 days in 2008: mean close to median, conversion parity exact at the mode and about 5% off at the mean, price higher where the relevant level band is larger. Small sample.
- Holm & Mäkinen token-price multipliers around Legion (figures verified; interpretation untested).
- Old School RuneScape item-sink and tax effects (Hogan-Hennessy et al., verified in full text; preprint, other game).
- Old School RuneScape index changes and volume concentration (Scholten et al., verified in full text; the paper's unit-root statements are internally inconsistent and should not be used).
- EVE Online Q1 2010 index changes and Laspeyres method (CCP, verified in full text).
- Retail mechanics from Blizzard's own posts: commodities bought by quantity from the cheapest listings, silver/gold pricing, region-wide commodities since 9.2.7, hourly auction API snapshots, Warband bank transfers.
- Forever, from Blizzard staff posts only: the 10 November 2026 hotfix items (quest gold at 48+, two vendor-value cuts, two ammo items tradeable) and the deferral of auction-house changes to the 17 November maintenance.
- Hardware-event requirement for `C_AuctionHouse.PlaceBid`, and the 15-minute account-wide throttle on `ReplicateItems` (API reference wiki, pages list a Forever build).
- TradeSkillMaster's own definitions of its market-value algorithm and price sources (tool documenting itself).
