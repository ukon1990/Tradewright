# Gold farming in World of Warcraft (earning gold by playing) and how players find and compare farms — notes as of 2026-11-20

Source tiers used below: [peer-reviewed], [working paper], [official], [primary data], [reference wiki], [tool docs] (addon documentation and source code), [community]. Every link was opened during this research unless the line says "NOT OPENED" or "partially opened". Every gold-per-hour figure from a guide is marked "self-reported, unverified". "Retail" = Midnight 12.x; "Classic" = Classic Era / Anniversary / progression Classic; "Forever" = the client flavor listed as "Forever 1.60.1" on warcraft.wiki.gg and CurseForge.

Terminology warning: in the academic literature "gold farming" means producing in-game goods for real-money sale (RMT), which Blizzard prohibits. In player usage, and in these notes, "farming" means earning in-game gold by playing. Heeks defines it as "the real-world sale of virtual goods and services produced in online games" — [Heeks 2008, p.2](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/gdi/publications/workingpapers/di/di_wp32.pdf) [working paper].

## Taxonomy of farms

### Takeaway
No authoritative, citable taxonomy of player farms exists; the closest academic one is Heeks's role list (hunter, gatherer, producer). Verified sources support these categories: gathering (herb, ore, skinning, fishing), open-world mob grinding (cloth, elemental drops, pets), solo dungeon farms, legacy raid raw-gold runs, hyperspawn grinding, and paid in-game services such as boosting.

### Cited Findings
- Academic role taxonomy of what farmers do in game: "Hunter" (kills NPCs, takes currency, sells drops), "Fighter", "Gatherer" (non-fighting resource gathering such as mining), "Producer" (crafting for sale), "Banker", "Dealer", "Marketer", "Leveller". Applies to MMOs generally, written 2008 about RMT farms — [Heeks 2008, pp.6-7](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/gdi/publications/workingpapers/di/di_wp32.pdf) [working paper]
- Heeks describes the base loop as killing monsters that "drop loot" (currency and valuable items) plus "mining, fishing or crafting virtual items" and trading — [Heeks 2008, p.2 Box 1](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/gdi/publications/workingpapers/di/di_wp32.pdf) [working paper]
- Gathering — herbalism (Retail Midnight): herbs are Tranquility Bloom, Argentleaf, Azeroot, Mana Lily, Sanguithorn, Nocturnal Lotus. "All herbs can now be gathered in all Midnight zones." Each herb has normal, Lush and four modified node variants (Lightfused in Eversong Woods, Wild in Zul'Aman, Primal in Harandar, Voidbound in Voidstorm), which yield motes (Mote of Light, Wild Magic, Primal Energy, Pure Void). Nocturnal Lotus has no node and is a rare by-product. Guide dated 5 March 2026 — [Method.gg Midnight Herbalism guide](https://www.method.gg/guides/midnight-herbalism-profession-guide) [community]
- Gathering — mining (Retail Midnight): Refulgent Copper Ore, Umbral Tin Ore, Brilliant Silver Ore, Dazzling Thorium. All ore in all Midnight zones; variants normal, Rich, Seam plus the same four zone modifiers. Dazzling Thorium has no node and is a rare by-product. Guide dated 5 March 2026 — [Method.gg Midnight Mining guide](https://www.method.gg/guides/midnight-mining-profession-guide) [community]
- Dual gathering (Retail Midnight): one character with Herbalism and Mining flies a loop. Mounted gathering needs 40 points in Botany and 40 in Meticulous Mining for non-Druids; Druids gather herbs in Flight Form. Zul'Aman Wild nodes spawn mobs. Published 3 July 2026, updated 17 Nov 2026. The site sells gold and boosts, so treat it as a commercial source — [Gamingcy dual gathering](https://gamingcy.com/blog/midnight-dual-gathering) [community]
- Gathering — skinning (Classic): Wetlands raptors (Medium Leather, level 27+), Swamp of Sorrows jaguars (Heavy/Thick Leather, 37+), Felwood bears (Thick/Rugged Leather, 50+), Eastern Plaguelands plaguebats (Rugged Leather, 56+), Chimaeroks in Feralas (Chimaerok Tenderloin, level 60 plus a partner). Undated guide — [Warcraft Tavern Classic gold guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/guides/wow-classic-gold-guide/) [community]
- Gathering — herbalism (Classic): Gromsblood in Felwood (skill 240), Ghost Mushroom in The Hinterlands (245), Dreamfoil in Azshara (270), Mountain Silversage in Winterspring (280), Black Lotus in Silithus (300) — [Warcraft Tavern Classic gold guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/guides/wow-classic-gold-guide/) [community]
- Gathering — fishing (Classic): Oily Blackmouth in Darkshore (skill 55), Firefin Snapper in Wetlands (130), Stonescale Eel in Stranglethorn Vale (205) — [Warcraft Tavern Classic gold guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/guides/wow-classic-gold-guide/) [community]
- Open-world mob grinding (Classic): cloth (Mageweave, Runecloth, Felcloth) from Felwood furbolgs and satyrs at 52+; grey and white items to vendor; rare pet drops (Tiny Crimson Whelpling in Wetlands, Tiny Emerald Whelpling in Swamp of Sorrows, Dark Whelpling in Badlands) at a stated 1-in-1,000 rate. The guide's own arithmetic: about 4 hours for 1,000 kills at 15 s each, with a 36.77% chance of no drop — [Warcraft Tavern Classic gold guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/guides/wow-classic-gold-guide/) [community]
- Open-world elemental farms (Classic Era): Searing Gorge elementals (Elemental Fire), Silithus air elementals (Essence of Air), Small Flame Sacs from Feralas sprites or whelps, Hearthglen cloth — [NoobToBoss mage guide](https://noobtoboss.com/wow-classic-mage-gold-farming-guide/) [community]
- Solo dungeon farms (Classic Era, mage): Dire Maul East lashers (herbs, vendor trash, Librams), DM East full clear (adds Rich Thorium veins), Zul'Farrak graveyard (40-60 Mageweave per clear plus vendor trash), Maraudon Ghost Mushroom run (no combat), Zul'Gurub crocodiles (bijous, coins), Scarlet Monastery Cathedral (vendor trash, greens for disenchanting), BRD arena — [NoobToBoss mage guide](https://noobtoboss.com/wow-classic-mage-gold-farming-guide/) [community]

- Legacy Loot Mode (Retail) turns on when the player is 10 or more levels above the content's maximum level; bosses then drop the maximum number of items (typically 5) from the whole loot table instead of a roughly 20% chance at one spec-eligible item. Introduced in the 8.0.1 pre-patch; as of 12.0.1 all instances through Dragonflight are in Legacy Loot Mode — [warcraft.wiki.gg Legacy loot](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Legacy_loot) [reference wiki]
- Battle for Azeroth raids are called the most profitable for raw gold per hour because BfA weapons vendor for more; the author recommends running every difficulty including LFR. No numbers given. Dated 7 Aug 2025 — [TwistedVoxel legacy raid tips](https://twistedvoxel.com/best-tips-and-tricks-for-legacy-raids-in-world-of-warcraft/) [community]

- A Midnight hyperspawn tied to one quest phase was used for level 80-90 boosting and gold; the article warns it may be an exploit and notes Blizzard has adjusted such spots quickly and sometimes punished players (Legion Remix frogs cited). Dated 18 May 2026 — [PC Games (DE)](https://www.pcgames.de/World-of-Warcraft-Spiel-42971/News/WoW-Hyperspawn-Speedleveln-moeglicher-Exploit-1534956/) [community]
- Services (Classic): mage portals and food, rogue lockpicking, tipped crafting, paid leveling help and dungeon runs — [Warcraft Tavern Classic gold guide](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/guides/wow-classic-gold-guide/) [community]
- Rare spawns, treasures and events are a trackable category: RareScanner tracks "rare NPCs, treasures/containers, and events" and has a loot explorer listing what each can drop with drop chances — [RareScanner on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarescanner) [tool docs]

### Inferences
- A farm can be modelled as (activity type, location, requirements, output item set, raw-gold component, cap type). The cap type differs by category: node respawn and competition for gathering, kill speed for grinding, instance entry limits for dungeon farms, weekly lockout for raids.
- In Midnight, base herbs and ores are not zone-locked, so zone choice is driven by modifier type (motes), terrain and mob density, not by which herb is wanted. Classic farms are strongly zone-locked.

### Gaps
- No verified source found for: reputation or vendor-recipe farms, transmog farming, world-quest and weekly raw-gold sources in Midnight, treasure or chest farming routes, and fishing pool farms in Midnight. These categories are commonly discussed but nothing above tier [community] was located, and no community page covering them was opened.
- The Warcraft Tavern page was truncated at 100,000 characters; its dungeon and boosting sections were not read.

## How gold per hour is measured, which price source values loot, and pitfalls

### Takeaway
Session trackers compute (value of looted items + raw coin) divided by active session time, where item value comes from a user-chosen price source (TSM market or region averages, Auctionator, Oribos Exchange) with a vendor-price fallback. The figure is an asset estimate based on listing prices, not realised income, and no tracker reviewed discounts for sale rate, auction house cut, or the farmer's own supply.

### Cited Findings
- LootAppraiser Reloaded per-hour formula (source code): delta = (pause start or now) − session start − accumulated pause seconds; per hour = total looted item value / delta × 3600 — [UI.lua, CalcLootedItemValuePerHour](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/UI/UI.lua) [tool docs]
- LootAppraiser Reloaded sessions store start time, map ID, the settings in force (quality filter, alert thresholds, price source), kills, total kills, looted item value ("liv"), group value and vendor-sold currency; pause time is subtracted — [Session.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/Session.lua) [tool docs]
- LootAppraiser Reloaded price sources (source code): Blizzard vendor price, TSM DBHistorical, DBMarket, DBRecent, DBMinBuyout, DBRegionHistorical, DBRegionMarketAvg, DBRegionSaleAvg, TSM VendorSell, a TSM custom price string, Oribos Exchange region median, Auctionator. The default is DBRegionMarketAvg; default quality filter is Common; default alert threshold 100 gold; random-enchant items ignored by default — [Const.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/Const.lua) [tool docs]
- LootAppraiser Reloaded valuation order (source code): skip items below the quality filter; skip blacklisted items (built-in list or a TSM group); get the price-source value; if that is 0 and quality is above Poor, fall back to the Blizzard vendor price (return 11 of GetItemInfo), or to TSM's "Destroy" value when the disenchant option is on; multiply by quantity. Raw coin is accumulated separately as totalLootedCurrency. No auction house cut or deposit adjustment appears in this file — [LootProcessing.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/LootProcessing.lua) [tool docs]
- LootAppraiser Reloaded supports per-item-class price source overrides (Armor, Consumable, Recipe, Tradeskill, Weapon, Quest) — [PriceSources.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/PriceSources.lua) [tool docs]
- LootAppraiser Reloaded v3.5.3 (13 Nov 2026) lists flavors Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Titan Reforged Classic, Classic; describes its output as "potential 'asset' values of items looted" — [LootAppraiser Reloaded on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lootappraiser-reloaded) [tool docs]
- The original LootAppraiser states "this addon does NOT determine liquid gold you are guaranteed to make"; soulbound and grey items are valued at vendor sell price; it was last updated 9 May 2024 for retail 10.2.7 and is discontinued in favour of the Reloaded fork — [LootAppraiser on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lootappraiser) [tool docs]
- MoneyLooter tracks raw gold and looted item value; gold per hour uses an exponential moving average with an adaptive alpha in the first 30 seconds (exact formula not published on the page). One price source is queried (TSM default with custom string `dbmarket`, or Auctionator, Auctioneer, OribosExchange, RECrystallize); if it returns nothing the vendor price is used — [MoneyLooter on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/moneylooter) [tool docs]
- MoneyLooter valuation order: forced vendor price if set; non-gear uses direct price or vendor; gear uses the price only if it meets a per-quality minimum threshold, otherwise the disenchant value for disenchantable Uncommon/Rare/Epic items; vendor price last. v2.1.1 dated 15 Oct 2026; compatible with Retail, Forever, Classic Era, Hardcore, SoD, TBC, WotLK, Cataclysm, MoP — [MoneyLooter on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/moneylooter) [tool docs]
- TSM price source definitions: DBMinBuyout is "the lowest priced auction on your realms Auction House" as of the last update and may be 30+ minutes old; DBMarket is "a weighted 14-day average of the auctions listed" favouring the last 3 days and reflects listings, not sales; DBRecent uses only the latest snapshot; DBHistorical is a 60-day average of DBMarket; DBRegionMarketAvg averages DBMarket across the region; DBRegionSaleAvg is the region's average actual sale price from TSM Accounting data — [TSM: Which Price Sources can I use](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean) [tool docs]
- TSM Accounting (ledger) sources from the player's own history: avgBuy, avgSell, minSell, maxSell, minBuy, maxBuy, SmartAvgBuy. Internal sources: vendorSell ("the value of a particular item when you sell it to a vendor"), vendorBuy, Destroy ("the value of the items you would receive by destroying it, on average", defaulting to DBMarket), Crafting, matPrice — [TSM: Which Price Sources can I use](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean) [tool docs]
- TSM separates "Price Sources" (return gold) from "Value Sources" (return a number); DBRegionSaleRate and SaleRate are value sources from AuctionDB — [TSM: What are Custom Strings](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/addon/what-are-custom-strings) [tool docs]
- External sources usable in TSM strings: OERealm and OERegion (Oribos Exchange, retail only, updated twice a week), AtrValue (Auctionator, from the player's own scans), AHDBMinBid, AHDBMinBuyout, AucMarket — [TSM: external sources](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/which-external-sources-are-available-to-use) [tool docs]
- FarmHud turns the minimap into a HUD with a gather circle and integrates with GatherMate2, Routes and HandyNotes; its page mentions no gold valuation. Last update 7 Nov 2026; flavors include Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Classic. Its ping function is disabled on Midnight because of ADDON_ACTION_FORBIDDEN — [FarmHud on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/farmhud) [tool docs]
- GatherMate2's collector records node type, node name and player map position on gathering spell casts (Mining, Herb Gathering, Fishing, Extract Gas, Opening, Archaeology, Logging). It records no loot quantities and no timestamps; LOOT and combat-log handlers are commented out — [GatherMate2 Collector.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nevcairiel/GatherMate2/master/Collector.lua) [tool docs]
- Auction house costs (Retail; page flagged outdated): the house takes 5% of the winning bid; the deposit is 15%, 30% or 60% of the vendor sell price for 12, 24 or 48 hours, with a 1 silver minimum; the deposit is lost on an unsold or cancelled auction — [warcraft.wiki.gg Auction House](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auction_House) [reference wiki]
- Precedent for time-valuing play: Castronova (2001), "Virtual Worlds: A First-Hand Account of Market and Society on the Cyberian Frontier", CESifo Working Paper No. 618, reports for EverQuest "The nominal hourly wage is about USD 3.42 per hour" and a currency exchange rate of USD 0.0107 per unit — [EconPapers record](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:ces:ceswps:_618) [working paper]
- A player reported legacy-raid gold estimates were about 50% above what they actually received — search-result snippet from a Blizzard EU forum thread, NOT OPENED, unverified — [EU forum thread](https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/updated-wow-gold-farming-for-new-players-no-ah-needed/96241) [community]

### Inferences
- Three distinct numbers hide behind "gold per hour": raw coin per hour (exact), vendor-floor value per hour (exact, from sellPrice), and auction value per hour (estimate). An addon should show them separately, since only the first two are certain.
- Pitfalls implied by the definitions above: (1) DBMarket and DBMinBuyout are listing prices, so unsold or overpriced listings inflate value; (2) a single high-priced listing of a rarely sold item produces a large phantom gain; (3) no tracker reviewed subtracts the 5% cut or lost deposits; (4) none models the price drop caused by selling the farmer's own volume; (5) soulbound loot has only vendor or disenchant value.
- A stricter valuation is possible with existing concepts: use a sales-based source (region sale average or the player's own ledger avgSell) where available, apply 0.95 for the cut, apply a per-quality minimum threshold with vendor/disenchant fallback as MoneyLooter does, and compare listed quantity against the session's output.
- Pause handling matters: LootAppraiser subtracts paused time and MoneyLooter smooths with an EMA. Travel, vendoring and mailing time are part of a farm's real cost and should stay inside the timed session.
- GatherMate2 node data alone cannot yield gold per hour because it stores positions only; yields per node and nodes per hour must come from the player's own session log.

### Gaps
- TSM's definitions of DBRegionSaleRate and DBRegionSoldPerDay were not on the pages opened; the exact formula and time window are unconfirmed.
- No TSM Ledger documentation page was opened; ledger behaviour is inferred only from the Accounting price-source definitions.
- No peer-reviewed study of gold-per-hour measurement accuracy in WoW was found. Castronova's wage figure is for EverQuest in 2001 and values play in USD, not in-game currency.
- The sale-rate pitfall (low sale rate items overvalued) is widely asserted in community discussion but no source defining or measuring it was opened.

## How experienced farmers choose what to farm

### Takeaway
Verified guidance is thin and mostly community-level: check current auction prices before choosing, match the farm to class, level, gear and professions, and plan around entry caps and competition. Blizzard's own posts confirm that instance caps and layer-change cooldowns exist specifically to limit repetitive farming.

### Cited Findings
- Price-first selection: a Midnight gathering guide declines to state a rate ("We give no gold per hour number") because prices vary by realm and week, and tells readers to check their own auction house before choosing what to farm — [Gamingcy dual gathering](https://gamingcy.com/blog/midnight-dual-gathering) [community]
- Requirements gate farms (Classic Era, mage): DM East lashers need a Frost AoE spec and benefit from Herbalism; ZG crocodiles need T1/T2-level gear; the Maraudon mushroom run needs no combat and any gear — [NoobToBoss mage guide](https://noobtoboss.com/wow-classic-mage-gold-farming-guide/) [community]
- Requirements gate farms (Retail Midnight): mounted gathering requires 40 knowledge points per profession for non-Druids, and some zones need "decent gear" because modified nodes spawn mobs — [Gamingcy dual gathering](https://gamingcy.com/blog/midnight-dual-gathering) [community]
- Competition: the same guide advises turning around if another gatherer is flying ahead on the route; the Classic guide notes competition at peak hours for Searing Gorge elementals — [Gamingcy dual gathering](https://gamingcy.com/blog/midnight-dual-gathering) [community]; [NoobToBoss mage guide](https://noobtoboss.com/wow-classic-mage-gold-farming-guide/) [community]
- Instance cap, Classic (official, 16 June 2020): "You may now enter a maximum of 30 unique instances (dungeon and raid) per day, per realm", alongside "the current limit of 5 instances per hour"; "This check is across all of your characters on your realm"; rationale: "ongoing efforts to eliminate exploitative and automated gameplay" — [Blizzard forum post by Kaivax](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/556616/1) [official]
- Instance cap, Retail: "A player can only enter ten individual instances in one hour", per account; each reset counts as a new instance; Follower Dungeons have a separate cap of 50 per day per account; the limit rose from 5 to 10 per hour in a 7 April 2014 hotfix. The page also states Classic and modern modes share the 10-per-hour limit as of the Midnight era, and that Classic formerly had different limits including a daily cap — [warcraft.wiki.gg Instance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Instance) [reference wiki]; conflicts with the 2020 Classic figures in [Kaivax's post](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/556616/1) [official] unless the Classic limits were later changed
- LootAppraiser Reloaded's settings text refers to "the 10 instance resets per hour" for its lockout display — [Config.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/Config.lua) [tool docs]
- An undated Classic Era guide still plans around a "five-instance-per-hour lockout" (25-40 lasher kills per hour before the cap; ZF runs of 8-12 minutes) — [NoobToBoss mage guide](https://noobtoboss.com/wow-classic-mage-gold-farming-guide/) [community]
- Raid lockouts: killing a boss creates a lock; raid locks reset Tuesday mornings and heroic dungeon locks daily at 4:00 server time — [warcraft.wiki.gg Instance](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Instance) [reference wiki]
- Layer hopping, Classic (official, 8 Sept 2019): after a layer change "there will be an increasing amount of time before that player can move to another layer", a hotfix to restrict frequent layer-hopping "without impacting those who simply wish to play with their friends" — [Blizzard forum post by Kaivax](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/290703) [official]
- Route planning tools: Routes builds a path through GatherMate2 nodes for chosen node types, optimises it as a travelling salesman problem with ant colony optimisation, clusters nearby nodes and supports "out of bounds" taboo regions. v1.9.1 dated 18 Nov 2026; flavors Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Classic, Classic TBC — [Routes on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/routes) [tool docs]

### Inferences
- The maximum income of an instance farm is capped at (value per run) × (runs allowed per hour), so the hourly cap is a first-class parameter. Because sources disagree on the current Classic number (5 versus 10 per hour, and whether a 30 per day cap still applies), the addon should treat the cap as configurable per flavor and should not hard-code it.
- A sensible ranking score for a candidate farm is expected output per hour × sale-based price × sell-through adjustment, filtered by the character's level, class, professions and knowledge points, then compared against the player's own logged sessions for the same farm.
- Layer or shard hopping to multiply node spawns is throttled by Blizzard and should not be a feature the addon assists.

### Gaps
- No verified source was opened for timing strategies (new patch, raid-day demand cycles) or for rotating farms to avoid flooding a market; these remain unsourced community lore here.
- Blizzard support article 10994 ("You Have Entered Too Many Instances Recently") could not be opened: [the US URL](https://us.battle.net/support/en/article/10994) redirects to a Battle.net login. A search-engine extract indicated 10 per hour per account for retail and 10 per hour for Classic in the EU copy, which is unverified.
- No official statement on instance limits for the Forever flavor or for Classic Anniversary realms was found.
- The Icy Veins Classic goldmaking guide returned HTTP 403 and was not read.

## Where structured item-source data lives

### Takeaway
Drop and gathering-source tables are not exposed with rates by the game client: the in-game APIs give vendor price, item class, bind type and boss loot lists without drop chances. Rates and node locations come from crowd-sourced collection (Wowhead's Looter addon, GatherMate2 data dumps, RareScanner's database) or from wiki tables, so an addon must ship a curated table or learn from the player's own sessions.

### Cited Findings
- In-game vendor price: `C_Item.GetItemInfo` returns `sellPrice` as return 11, "The vendor price in copper, or `0` for items that cannot be sold", plus quality, classID, subclassID, bindType and isCraftingReagent. Listed for Retail 12.1.5, Forever 1.60.1, MoP Classic 5.5.4, TBC Classic 2.5.6, Vanilla Classic 1.15.9. Returns nil until the item is cached — [warcraft.wiki.gg API C_Item.GetItemInfo](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_Item.GetItemInfo) [reference wiki]
- In-game boss loot: the Encounter Journal loot API returns an item info table with itemID, encounterID, name, quality, slot, armorType, link and display flags (displayAsVeryRare, displayAsExtremelyRare). It contains no drop-rate field. Listed for Retail 12.1.5, Forever 1.60.1, MoP Classic 5.5.4, TBC 2.5.6, Vanilla 1.15.9. The exact function name on the page should be re-checked before coding against it — [warcraft.wiki.gg, page reached via API_EJ_GetLootInfoByIndex](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_EJ_GetLootInfoByIndex) [reference wiki]
- Wowhead's database is crowd-sourced from players: the Wowhead Client "maintains a WoW addon called the Wowhead Looter, which collects data as you play the game" and uploads it — [Wowhead Classic item page for Black Lotus](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=13468/black-lotus) [primary data]
- Wowhead's per-item XML feed exposes id, name, quality, class and subclass, level, a `sellprice` (1000 copper for Black Lotus), an `avgbuyout` value and a numeric `source` code array, but no dropped-by list — [Wowhead item XML](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=13468&xml) [primary data]
- GatherMate2 tracks "herbs, mines, fishing pools, gas clouds, archaelogy finds and treasures"; licence GPL v2 or later — [GatherMate2 on GitHub](https://github.com/Nevcairiel/GatherMate2) [tool docs]
- GatherMate2_Data is a "Wowhead Data dump for GatherMate2 for Retail and Forever", "sourced from WoWHead and other players", imported in game through the GatherMate2 Import section. "Classic is not included in this addon." v42.0 dated 13 Nov 2026 — [GatherMate2_Data on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/gathermate2_data) [tool docs]
- warcraft.wiki.gg has a per-expansion herb table with columns Node, skill thresholds (Orange, Yellow, Green, Gray), Normal Loot, Low-Skill Loot, Habitat and Zones found. Examples: Dreamfoil in "Level 45+ zones"; Black Lotus in Silithus, Blasted Lands, Winterspring, Eastern Plaguelands. The Midnight section is an empty stub as of this research — [warcraft.wiki.gg Herb](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Herb) [reference wiki]
- The Herbalism overview page maps expansions to proficiency names and skill ranges and links to the Herb table; it lists Classic and Forever herb navigation lists — [warcraft.wiki.gg Herbalism](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Herbalism) [reference wiki]
- RareScanner ships tracking for "every entity in the game" (rares, treasures, events) with a loot panel that includes drop chances; detection uses vignettes on retail and mostly nameplates on Classic versions including Forever. Last update 18 Nov 2026 — [RareScanner on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarescanner) [tool docs]
- HandyNotes is "a small and simple framework for showing notes on your map" with a plugin model for other authors' note sets. v1.6.32 dated 3 Nov 2026; flavors Retail, Forever, MoP Classic, Classic, Classic TBC — [HandyNotes on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes) [tool docs]
- Player-side observation APIs used by trackers: LootAppraiser Reloaded's kill tracker uses the combat log, `GetLootSourceInfo` and `GetNumLootItems` and de-duplicates kills by GUID within 120 seconds — [KillTracker.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/KillTracker.lua) [tool docs]

### Inferences
- Three data layers are available to an addon: (1) exact client data (vendor price, item class, bind type, boss loot lists); (2) shipped static tables (zone to herb/ore/skin/cloth mappings, hand-curated farm definitions, GatherMate2 node positions if GatherMate2 is installed); (3) learned data from the player's own sessions (drops per kill, nodes per hour, yield per node).
- Because drop rates cannot be queried in game, a "farm finder" needs either a shipped item-to-source table (curated from Wowhead or wiki pages, with licensing checked) or must start from the reverse direction: take high-value, high-volume auction items and map them to known source zones.
- GatherMate2_Data's GPL-licensed data and its explicit Forever coverage make an optional integration (read node counts per zone if GatherMate2 is loaded) more practical than shipping a node database.
- RareScanner demonstrates that shipping drop chances per NPC inside an addon is feasible; its data provenance was not verified.

### Gaps
- Wowhead's "Dropped by", "Gathered from" and "Contained in" tabs with counts and percentages could not be read by the tools used (the tabs are script-rendered and a direct download returned an empty body). The presence of sample-size columns is therefore unverified here.
- No Classic node database equivalent to GatherMate2_Data was verified; the opened page says Classic is excluded.
- Classic database sites (classicdb and similar) and skinning or fishing-pool tables on the wiki were not opened.
- Client database extracts (DB2 tables such as item sell prices or journal loot) were not checked; whether loot tables exist client-side is unconfirmed by an opened source.
- Licensing terms for redistributing Wowhead-derived data in an addon were not researched.

### Takeaway
All gold-per-hour numbers found are self-reported guide estimates with no methodology, sample size or date of measurement; the better guides now decline to give a number at all. They are useful only as relative ordering within one guide, never as absolute expectations.

- Searing Gorge elementals: 30-50 g/hour; Small Flame Sacs: 25-40 g/hour; Hearthglen: 40-60 g/hour; Silithus air elementals: 45-70 g/hour (self-reported, unverified)

Other examples without a rate:

- Classic Era guide figures cluster between 25 and 120 gold per hour, and the ordering (raid-zone and dungeon AoE farms above open-world elemental farms) is more durable than the numbers.

### Gaps
- Search results showed Midnight claims ranging from about 3,500 to more than 150,000 gold per hour on boosting and gold-selling sites and video-listing pages. Those pages were not opened or could not be verified, come from commercially interested sources, and are excluded.

- No verified examples were found for retail open-world cloth farms, transmog runs, reputation recipe farms, pet or mount farms, or for the Forever flavor specifically.

## Raw gold versus auction-house-dependent income, and vendor-trash valuation

### Takeaway
Raw coin and vendor value are deterministic and can be computed exactly from client data; auction value is an estimate that depends on which price source is used, whether the item actually sells, and a 5% cut plus possibly forfeited deposits. Trackers handle this by falling back to vendor price when no market price exists.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor value is exact client data: `sellPrice` is "The vendor price in copper, or `0` for items that cannot be sold" — [warcraft.wiki.gg API C_Item.GetItemInfo](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/API_C_Item.GetItemInfo) [reference wiki]
- Wowhead's item feed carries the same vendor price (Black Lotus `sellprice` 1000 copper, shown as 10 silver) — [Wowhead item XML](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/item=13468&xml) [primary data]
- Auction proceeds formula: "Amount Received = Winning Bid - Cut + Deposit" with a 5% cut; unsold auctions forfeit a deposit of 15/30/60% of vendor price for 12/24/48 hours (page flagged outdated; no Classic-specific fee data on it) — [warcraft.wiki.gg Auction House](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auction_House) [reference wiki]
- Since patch 9.2.7 (16 Aug 2022) commodities such as herbs, gems and consumables share one region-wide listing pool on retail, while armor and weapons stay realm-specific — [warcraft.wiki.gg Auction House](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Auction_House) [reference wiki]
- Trackers value soulbound and grey loot at vendor price — [LootAppraiser on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lootappraiser) [tool docs]; and use vendor price whenever the market source returns nothing — [MoneyLooter on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/moneylooter) [tool docs]; [LootProcessing.lua](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/berickus/LootAppraiserReloaded/HEAD/Core/LootProcessing.lua) [tool docs]
- MoneyLooter adds a per-quality minimum price threshold below which gear is valued at disenchant value instead of market price, and has a vendor button that sells marked items 12 at a time so mistakes can be bought back — [MoneyLooter on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/moneylooter) [tool docs]
- TSM market sources describe listings, not completed sales, and min buyout can be 30+ minutes stale — [TSM price sources](https://support.tradeskillmaster.com/custom-strings/which-price-sources-can-i-use-and-what-do-they-mean) [tool docs]
- Legacy Loot Mode raises the quantity of vendorable boss loot for over-levelled characters (maximum item count per boss) — [warcraft.wiki.gg Legacy loot](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Legacy_loot) [reference wiki]
- Trackers disclaim the auction figure as an asset estimate: "this addon does NOT determine liquid gold you are guaranteed to make" — [LootAppraiser on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/lootappraiser) [tool docs]

### Inferences
- A reliable per-item value rule: value = max(vendor price, auction price × 0.95) only when the auction price comes from a trusted source and the item is tradeable (bindType is not bind-on-pickup); otherwise vendor price (or disenchant value if the player can disenchant).
- Raw-gold farms (legacy raids, vendor-trash dungeon runs) give a floor that does not depend on market conditions or scan freshness, which makes them the right baseline to compare auction-dependent farms against.
- On Forever and Classic-style realms without region-wide commodity pools, the player's own realm scan is the only local price source, so the staleness and thin-listing problems are larger than on retail.

### Gaps
- No verified source quantifies how often farmed items fail to sell, or how vendor prices of old raid loot have been adjusted across expansions (a forum claim that Blizzard reduces them each expansion appeared only in a search snippet and was not opened).
- Classic and Forever auction fee rules (cut and deposit by faction or neutral house) were not verified.

## Which farming-related actions are against Blizzard's rules

### Takeaway
Automation (bots), exploits, input broadcasting across multiple clients, real-money sale of farmed gold or items, and paid boosting for real payment are all prohibited; addons must be free, readable and compliant with the EULA. A farm-finder addon that only displays information and logs the player's own loot fits within these rules.

### Cited Findings
- Blizzard EULA (last revised 21 March 2024), section 1.C.ii prohibits "cheats; i.e. methods not expressly authorized by Blizzard" including exploits of in-game bugs, and "bots; i.e. any code and/or software, not expressly authorized by Blizzard, that allows the automated control of a Game" — [Blizzard End User License Agreement](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/fba4d00f-c7e4-4883-b8b9-1b4500a402ea/blizzard-end-user-license-agreement) [official]
- EULA section 1.C.iii prohibits "gathering in-game currency, items, or resources for sale outside of the Platform or the Game(s)" and "performing in-game services including, without limitation, account boosting or power-leveling, in exchange for payment" — [Blizzard End User License Agreement](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/fba4d00f-c7e4-4883-b8b9-1b4500a402ea/blizzard-end-user-license-agreement) [official]
- EULA section 1.C.vi prohibits unauthorized software that "intercepts, collects, reads, or 'mines' information" from the platform — [Blizzard End User License Agreement](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/legal/fba4d00f-c7e4-4883-b8b9-1b4500a402ea/blizzard-end-user-license-agreement) [official]
- Input broadcasting (posted 3 November; the linked forum announcement is dated 3 November 2020): software that "mirrors keystrokes to multiple WoW game clients will soon be considered an actionable offense"; warnings first, then actions up to "permanent closure of the player's World of Warcraft account(s)" — [Blizzard news post](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23558957/policy-update-for-input-broadcasting-software) [official]; date from [forum announcement by Kaivax](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/policy-update-for-input-broadcasting-software/707427) [official]
- Extension (5 May 2021): Blizzard will "prohibit the use of all software and hardware mechanisms to mirror commands to multiple World of Warcraft accounts" and efforts "to automate or streamline multi-boxing in any way" — [Blizzard forum post by Kaivax](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/policy-update-for-input-broadcasting-may-2021/956610/1) [official]
- UI Add-On Development Policy: add-ons must be free of charge, have completely visible code, must not negatively impact realms or other players, may not include advertisements or solicit donations, and must abide by the ToU and EULA; Blizzard may disable add-on functionality — [Blizzard UI Add-On Development Policy](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ui-add-on-development-policy/24534) [official]
- Blizzard ties instance caps directly to enforcement: the 30-per-day Classic cap was introduced "As part of our ongoing efforts to eliminate exploitative and automated gameplay" — [Blizzard forum post by Kaivax](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/556616/1) [official]
- Game companies do not permit bots or macroing, and RMT farms have been reported to use them — [Heeks 2008, p.6 Box 2](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/gdi/publications/workingpapers/di/di_wp32.pdf) [working paper]
- Scale of the prohibited RMT trade (2008 estimates the author calls best guesses): about US$500m per year, unlikely below US$200m and possibly above US$1bn; employment between 150,000 and 400,000; monthly salary about US$145 — [Heeks 2008, p.10](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/gdi/publications/workingpapers/di/di_wp32.pdf) [working paper]
- Hyperspawn farming is a grey area: players point to the EULA's exploit clause, and press coverage warns the Midnight hyperspawn "may be an exploit" with past adjustments and punishments — [Blizzard forum thread (player posts)](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/what-are-the-rules-on-hypserspawn-farming/583706) [community]; [PC Games (DE), 18 May 2026](https://www.pcgames.de/World-of-Warcraft-Spiel-42971/News/WoW-Hyperspawn-Speedleveln-moeglicher-Exploit-1534956/) [community]
- Even permitted addons hit protected-action limits: FarmHud's ping function is disabled on Midnight due to ADDON_ACTION_FORBIDDEN — [FarmHud on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/farmhud) [tool docs]

### Inferences
- Compliant scope for the feature: read prices, log the player's own loot and time, display routes or suggestions. Out of scope: any automated movement, targeting, looting or selling without a hardware click, anything that assists multi-client input mirroring, and any link to real-money sale.
- The addon should not recommend or rank hyperspawn spots or bug-dependent farms, since Blizzard treats exploits as cheats and has acted on such spots.
- In-game gold-for-service boosting is a separate question from the EULA's "in exchange for payment" clause; the opened text does not say whether in-game gold counts as payment, so boosting should be treated cautiously and not promoted as a farm type.

### Gaps
- Lehdonvirta and Ernkvist (2011), "Knowledge Map of the Virtual Economy", World Bank / infoDev, Report No. 27361: citation confirmed at [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:wbk:wboper:27361) [working paper], but the full text returned HTTP 403, so no findings are taken from it.
- Ahmad, Keegan, Srivastava, Williams and Contractor (2009), "Mining for Gold Farmers: Automatic Detection of Deviant Players in MMOGs", IEEE conference paper, pp. 340-345 (EverQuest II data): NOT OPENED. [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/5284055) returned an empty page and mirrors returned HTTP 403; details come from a search snippet only and the DOI is unconfirmed [peer-reviewed].
- No current Blizzard statement was found on whether in-game boosting for gold is permitted on each flavor, nor an official definition of hyperspawn exploitation.
- Blizzard's separate support articles on multiboxing and third-party software were not opened; the EULA and the two policy posts are the only official texts relied on.
